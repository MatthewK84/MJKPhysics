# Validation

Validation answers one question for each model: is the implementation computing what its page
says it computes, to a stated tolerance? Checks fall into four levels.

| Level | Compares against | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Analytic | Closed-form solutions | Free fall, projectile with linear drag, torque-free precession, the intermediate-axis flip, bounce height $`e^2 h`$, sliding on an incline |
| Numerical | Properties every correct method must have | Measured convergence order, energy and angular-momentum drift, unit-norm preservation on SO(3) |
| Published data and reference code | Measurements and independent implementations | UIUC propeller data, the Crazyflie identification, Aerosonde trim and eigenvalues from BYU's reference code, the NTNU Skywalker X8 papers |
| Statistical | Spectra and noise statistics | Turbulence power spectra against the Dryden model, IMU noise recovered from Allan deviation |

Every check reports four things:

- **Tolerance**, and why it suits the step size and model.
- **Result**: the measured error.
- **Pass or fail.**
- **Known limitations**: what the check doesn't cover.

The tables on this page are generated by `mjk validate`, so they always match the code that
produced them. The first results arrive with the analytic suite
([#16](https://github.com/MatthewK84/MJKPhysics/issues/16)) and the command-line tool
([#17](https://github.com/MatthewK84/MJKPhysics/issues/17)). The full report is
[#81](https://github.com/MatthewK84/MJKPhysics/issues/81).

## Verified so far

The model checks above begin with the rigid-body core. The foundations they rest on are already
tested, each property over hundreds of random inputs and each edge case deterministically:

| Component | What is checked |
|---|---|
| [Frame-typed math](../math-types.md) ([#8](https://github.com/MatthewK84/MJKPhysics/issues/8)) | Rotations preserve norms; a quaternion times its inverse is the identity; quaternion composition matches the matrix product; transpose, determinant, inverse and symmetric eigen-decomposition identities; exact results of the elementary operators; eigen-decomposition accuracy independent of scale and of off-diagonal patterns |
| [SO(3) maps](../so3.md) ([#9](https://github.com/MatthewK84/MJKPhysics/issues/9)) | Log inverts Exp for angles from $`10^{-12}`$ rad to just under $`\pi`$; Exp inverts Log and takes the short way; Jacobian identities, and the right Jacobian linearizes Exp; plus and minus invert each other; slerp moves at constant rate; Euler angles round-trip, including at gimbal lock; matrix conversion round-trips and non-rotations are rejected; Jacobian coefficients agree with 40-digit references; the small-angle branches of Exp and Log are accurate to within 2 and 4 ulp |
| [Rigid-body dynamics](../models/rigid-body.md) ([#10](https://github.com/MatthewK84/MJKPhysics/issues/10)) | Composite mass properties against a hand-computed tensor and the shape formulas; cutting a component in half or rotating every component leaves the result consistent; Euler's equations against their principal-axis closed form; power balance and conservation of angular momentum hold for any state, inertia and loads |
| [Integrators](../models/integrators.md) ([#11](https://github.com/MatthewK84/MJKPhysics/issues/11), [#12](https://github.com/MatthewK84/MJKPhysics/issues/12)) | Each method against its closed form under constant acceleration; RK4 exact for cubic rates; RKMK4 exact for steady principal-axis spin; measured order 1, 1, 4 and 4 (within ±0.2) on an oscillator and on a tumbling body; semi-implicit Euler energy bounded over 10⁶ steps while explicit Euler's grows as predicted; RKMK4 keeps a unit quaternion with no renormalization; the RK4-against-RKMK4 study table is generated by `mjk validate` and checked against the code |
| [Atmosphere and gravity](../models/atmosphere.md) ([#15](https://github.com/MatthewK84/MJKPhysics/issues/15)) | Temperature, pressure, density, speed of sound and viscosity match the 1976 standard's printed tables at 0, 1, 2, 5, 11 and 20 km to four significant figures (largest gap 0.10 of a unit in the fourth figure), in a table generated by `mjk validate atmosphere`; the air is continuous at the tropopause and in hydrostatic balance with any temperature offset; WGS-84 normal gravity matches the exact field of the level ellipsoid to $`1.5\times10^{-6}`$ m/s² from −5 to 20 km, and its derived constants follow from the four defining parameters |
| [World loop](../architecture.md#one-simulation-step) ([#13](https://github.com/MatthewK84/MJKPhysics/issues/13)) | Every subsystem runs exactly at its rate over 10 s (500, 250, 50 and 10 Hz at 1 kHz); sensors run before controllers within a tick; commands are held between controller updates; time is exact at tick 123 456 789; the real-time accumulator carries remainders and drops a backlog it cannot catch up on; two runs are identical to the bit; a step allocates nothing |
| [Determinism and replay](../determinism.md) ([#14](https://github.com/MatthewK84/MJKPhysics/issues/14)) | `SplitMix64`, xoshiro256** and FNV-1a match their published reference algorithms; adding a random stream leaves the others unchanged; no two seed-and-name pairs share a stream, no state word is shared within a run, and streams start with different draws and are uncorrelated (weekly, PractRand on interleaved streams to 32 GB); uniform and normal samples have the right values and moments; two runs of the reference scenario are identical at every checkpoint; replaying its recorded inputs reproduces the final hash; changing one input changes every hash from its tick on; a WebAssembly build prints the same tables as the native build, byte for byte; recording and replay allocate nothing |
| Inertia consistency ([#7](https://github.com/MatthewK84/MJKPhysics/issues/7)) | Non-finite, asymmetric, indefinite and triangle-inequality-violating tensors are rejected, with each principal minor and its tolerance pinned by a case only it can detect ([Engineering standards](../engineering-standards.md#what-mutation-testing-found)) |
| [Configuration files](../configuration.md) ([#7](https://github.com/MatthewK84/MJKPhysics/issues/7)) | Each of 14 malformed sample files produces an error naming the exact field at fault |

These are unit and property tests, and their tolerances are stated in the tests. The generated
report ([#81](https://github.com/MatthewK84/MJKPhysics/issues/81)) will tabulate model checks in
the format above.

## How the tests are checked

Two gates in CI measure the tests themselves. Engine line coverage must stay at or above 90 %.
Mutation testing changes the code in small ways and fails if any change goes unnoticed by every
test; no mutant of the engine survives. Both are described on the
[Engineering standards](../engineering-standards.md#testing-the-tests) page.
