# Decision record template

Copy this into `docs/src/adr/NNNN-short-title.md`, add it to `SUMMARY.md` and to the index in
[Decision records](../adr/README.md).

````markdown
# ADR NNNN: Decision in a few words

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR NNNN | Rejected
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Issues:** links to the issues this decision affects

## Context

The problem, the forces at play and the constraints. Facts, not advocacy.

## Decision

What was decided, stated so someone could check whether the code follows it.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what must now be tested or monitored.

## Alternatives considered

- **Alternative.** Why it was not chosen, in one or two sentences.
````
